What it does

🩺

Comprehensive Logging

Log glucose readings, meals, symptoms, and insulin doses in seconds from a single unified entry screen.

📊

Dashboard Intelligence

Time in Range, estimated A1C, and Insulin on Board calculated from your logged data and displayed at a glance.

Clinical citations included
🤖

AI-Powered Insights

Pattern detection and plain-language analysis powered by Google Gemini. Free tier available — no subscription needed.

Free to set up
🔔

Pip — Smart Reminders

A companion that nudges you to log at the right times. Local notifications only — no glucose values, no treatment suggestions.

🆘

Emergency Contacts

Pre-fill an SMS to your emergency contacts on a severe reading. Confirmation countdown prevents accidental sends. Always free, always accessible.

Always free — no paywall
🔒

On-Device Privacy

All glucose, meal, and insulin data stays on your iPhone. No cloud sync, no server, no data selling. Health data never touches the ad SDK.
