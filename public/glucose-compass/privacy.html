🩺 Glucose Compass — iOS App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 14, 2026  ·  Last Updated: June 14, 2026

Our core commitment: Your health data is yours. Glucose Compass stores all glucose readings, meals, insulin logs, and symptoms locally on your device only. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose beyond making the app work for you.

1. Overview

Glucose Compass ("the app") is a personal health logging tool developed by Alex Bonacci ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Glucose Compass is designed for people with Type 1 Diabetes who want to track glucose readings, meals, insulin doses, and symptoms. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

3. Health Data — Special Protections

Glucose, insulin, meal, and symptom data are stored exclusively on your iPhone using iOS secure local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device except when you explicitly trigger an AI analysis query
  • Is never uploaded to our servers — we do not operate a server that receives your health data
  • Is never used for advertising, marketing, or analytics purposes
  • Is never sold to third parties
  • Is never shared with healthcare providers, insurers, or any third party without your explicit action

When you use the AI analysis feature, a snapshot of your recent glucose and meal data is sent to your configured AI provider (Google Gemini or your own API key) for that query only. AI providers do not retain health data beyond the scope of the individual request, per their published terms.

4. Advertising

Free-tier users see non-personalized ads served by Google AdMob. These ads:

  • Are non-personalized — requestNonPersonalizedAdsOnly: true is set on every ad request
  • Do not receive any health data — ad requests contain no glucose, meal, insulin, or symptom information
  • Do not use Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is not requested
  • Do not appear in any safety, emergency, or health-critical screen

Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" one-time purchase see no ads.

5. Emergency Contacts and Notifications

Emergency contact names and phone numbers you enter are stored locally on your device only. They are used exclusively to send alert notifications through iOS. This information is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Emergency alert features are permanently free. They are not affected by subscription status, ad preferences, or any other commercial element of the app.

6. Camera Access

Glucose Compass requests camera access to enable you to photograph your glucose meter or food labels when logging entries. Photos are used only for the logging session and are stored locally on your device. We do not upload photos to any server.

7. AI Features and Data Handling

AI features are optional and require you to configure an AI provider. The app supports:

  • Google Gemini (free tier): Authentication via Google OAuth. Data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): Your own API key is stored in iOS secure storage. Data sent to your chosen provider is subject to that provider's privacy policy.

All AI-generated content in the app includes a medical disclaimer. AI outputs are informational only and must not be used to make medical decisions. Always consult your healthcare provider.

8. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services receive limited data as part of app functionality:

  • Google AdMob — Non-personalized ad serving (free tier only). No health data shared. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • RevenueCat — Subscription and purchase management. Receives anonymized purchase receipts. RevenueCat Privacy Policy ↗
  • Expo / EAS — App distribution and anonymized crash reporting. Expo Privacy Policy ↗
  • Apple — In-App Purchase processing, TestFlight distribution, App Store delivery.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your health data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling Glucose Compass removes all locally stored glucose readings, logs, emergency contacts, and settings.

To request deletion of any data held by RevenueCat (purchase records) or Expo (crash logs), contact us at the address below and we will facilitate the request with those services.

10. Children's Privacy

Glucose Compass is rated 4+ on the App Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not include social features, user-generated content visible to others, or any mechanism for sharing data between users. If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will assist with deletion.

11. Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to know what personal data is collected about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of the sale of your data (we do not sell data)
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. Because health data is stored locally, we may direct you to delete it directly from your device.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app adds new features or third-party integrations. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We will notify users of material changes through the App Store release notes or an in-app notice.
